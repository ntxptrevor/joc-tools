# Field Closeout Checklist

Use this checklist during the final 10% of a Job Order Contracting or renovation project. Assign each item, attach evidence, and record exceptions before requesting final acceptance.

## Project record

- Project:
- Owner / facility:
- Job order / project number:
- Superintendent:
- Project manager:
- Substantial completion target:
- Final completion target:

## 1. Life safety and inspections

- [ ] Final building inspection passed
- [ ] Fire alarm and fire sprinkler acceptance completed
- [ ] Electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and specialty inspections closed
- [ ] Required certificates, permits, and inspection reports filed
- [ ] Temporary protection, barriers, and impairment notices removed
- [ ] Egress routes, exit signs, doors, and accessibility features verified

## 2. Punch and quality control

- [ ] Contractor pre-punch completed by superintendent
- [ ] Owner and designer punch items logged with responsible party and due date
- [ ] Completed punch items photographed before concealment or turnover
- [ ] Finishes cleaned, repaired, and protected
- [ ] Equipment labels, valve tags, panel schedules, and room signage installed
- [ ] Remaining exceptions documented and accepted in writing

## 3. Systems startup and training

- [ ] Startup reports and testing/adjusting/balancing reports received
- [ ] Controls sequences and alarm points verified
- [ ] Owner training completed with attendance record
- [ ] Keys, cores, access credentials, remotes, and special tools transferred
- [ ] Warranty start dates confirmed

## 4. Closeout documents

- [ ] Record drawings and field-marked changes collected
- [ ] O&M manuals indexed and delivered
- [ ] Product data, warranties, and subcontractor contacts included
- [ ] Approved submittals, RFIs, ASIs, and change documentation archived
- [ ] Lien waivers, consent of surety, payroll, HUB, and compliance documents complete
- [ ] Final schedule update and completion photographs saved

## 5. Commercial closeout

- [ ] Job-order scope and approved changes reconciled
- [ ] Allowances, unit-price work, and credits resolved
- [ ] Final quantities and owner-directed work documented
- [ ] Final invoice and retainage package submitted
- [ ] Open commitments, purchase orders, and subcontract balances reviewed

## 6. Turnover and demobilization

- [ ] Owner acceptance or substantial-completion document signed
- [ ] Temporary utilities, fencing, storage, dumpsters, and signage removed
- [ ] Adjacent areas restored and final cleaning accepted
- [ ] Owner property and spare materials inventoried and transferred
- [ ] Warranty callback process communicated
- [ ] Final completion confirmation recorded

## Evidence standard

For every checked item, retain at least one supporting record: inspection result, dated photograph, signed form, training roster, transmittal, email acceptance, or system report.
